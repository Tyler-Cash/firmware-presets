#$ DESCRIPTION: Doesn't position osd_core_temp_pos (FC temperature) even though there's screen room for it on HD - I don't have a known-good coordinate for it without risking overlapping another element. Position it via Configurator's OSD tab and export a fresh diff if you want it added here.
#$ DESCRIPTION: osd_rssi_dbm_alarm is provided as a packet-rate option below rather than a fixed value, since the right alarm threshold depends on the ELRS air rate configured on that craft's receiver, not on Betaflight or on pilot preference.
