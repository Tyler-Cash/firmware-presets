#$ DESCRIPTION: My personal switch layout, applied to every quad in my fleet so arming and mode behavior feel identical no matter which craft I'm flying.
#$ DESCRIPTION: AUX1 high (1900-2100) = ARM.
#$ DESCRIPTION: AUX7 is a 3-position switch: low (900-1200) = Position Hold, mid (1225-1650) = Angle, high (1700-2100) = Altitude Hold. Position Hold and Altitude Hold are inert on craft without GPS/baro - harmless, just unused.
#$ DESCRIPTION: AUX8 low (900-1025) = LEDLOW (dims/disables the LED strip in flight).
#$ DESCRIPTION: AUX2 high (1700-2100) = Turtle / Flip-over-after-crash.
#$ DESCRIPTION: The beeper auto-confirms mode changes: it beeps on entering Turtle, Angle, Altitude Hold, or Failsafe, and it auto-mutes whenever LEDLOW is engaged (so going stealthy also goes quiet).
#$ DESCRIPTION: Every other AUX slot is explicitly reset to disabled so this preset always leaves a clean, fully-known mode setup regardless of what was configured before.
